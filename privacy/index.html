Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

What We Collect

When you contact us directly — by email or through a contact form — we may collect your name and email address. Our apps collect usage data that varies by product; each app's onboarding describes what is gathered. We collect only what is necessary to operate our products.

How We Use It

We use the information we collect to respond to your inquiries, operate and improve our products, and communicate with you about your use of our software. We do not sell your personal data.

Third Parties

Our products are distributed through Apple and Google platforms, which have their own privacy practices. Our AI-powered features use services provided by Anthropic and Google AI; inputs to those features may be processed by those providers in accordance with their policies. We recommend reviewing their policies if you have questions about AI data handling.

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to operate our products. You may request deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at the address below, and we will honor that request within a reasonable time.
