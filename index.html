About Culminum

Culminum LLC was founded by Christopher DiMarco and Brian Alexander in Dallas, Texas. Christopher brings twenty years of architectural practice to the design of our tools — the kind of professional experience that keeps our software grounded in how work actually happens, not how software companies imagine it does.

We build for disciplines that leave no room for guesswork: architecture, documentation, compliance. And we build for the moments that matter outside work — daily reflection, personal clarity, faith.

Our tools amplify expertise. They don't replace the professional.
